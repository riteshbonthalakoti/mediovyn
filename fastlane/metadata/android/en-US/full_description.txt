MEDIOVYN is a high-performance native Android media player application designed for smooth video playback, audio equalizer customization, and secure media storage.

Key Features:
- Hybrid OLED Dark Theme with Electric Violet and Cyan Glow aesthetics.
- Material You Dynamic Colors support.
- 10-Band Equalizer & Volume Booster up to +15dB (~200%).
- Remote network streaming for SMB, FTP, WebDAV, HLS (.m3u8), and DASH (.mpd).
- OpenSubtitles subtitle hash matcher and downloader.
- Biometric AES-256 encrypted private video vault.
- Picture-in-Picture (PiP) & background audio playback.
